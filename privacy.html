本政策说明 Chrome 扩展「Mullion · 一屏多页」（以下简称“Mullion”或“我们”）如何收集、使用、存储和披露你的信息。使用 Mullion 即表示你已阅读本政策。

简而言之：不登录时，你的所有数据只保存在你的浏览器中，我们收不到任何信息。只有在你选择登录后，我们才会保存你的邮箱、网址配置和会员记录，用于同步和提供会员服务。我们不出售数据，不投放广告，不使用统计 SDK，也不采集或上传你浏览的网页内容。

1. 不登录时：数据只在本地

以下数据保存在当前 Chrome 配置文件的扩展本地存储（chrome.storage.local）中，不会发送给我们：

  • 工作台配置：你添加的网址、名称、顺序，以及兼容模式、外链处理、缩放和所选区域（元素定位信息与简短标签）等设置；
  • 界面状态，例如待授权的网址草稿。

工作台中的网页由你的浏览器直接从对应网站加载，这些网站如何处理你的数据由其自身的隐私政策决定。为了实现宽度适配、区域预览和外站链接在新标签页打开，Mullion 会在你授权的网站预览中运行扩展自带的脚本。这些脚本只在本地调整显示效果，不读取、不保存、也不上传网页内容。

书签：开启书签抽屉或命令面板中的书签搜索并授权后，Mullion 会在本地读取 Chrome 书签用于展示和搜索，不创建、修改或上传书签。网站图标只从 Chrome 本地缓存中读取，不会访问书签对应的网站。

嵌入检测：对已授权的网站，Mullion 会在本地读取工作台预览的响应头（例如 X-Frame-Options 和 Content-Security-Policy），判断网站是否拒绝被嵌入，从而在窗格中提示原因。这些信息只用于当次显示，不保存、不上传。窗格中的「反馈这个网站」会用你自己的邮件应用打开一封草稿，只包含网站的域名，是否发送由你决定。

站点授权范围：从 1.7 起，Mullion 为一个网站申请授权时，会覆盖它所在的整个网站，包括子域名和 http/https 两种协议（例如「xueqiu.com 的所有网站」）。网站的接口和登录服务常在子域名上，这样窗格才能带上你的登录状态。授权可以随时在 Chrome 的扩展设置中撤销。

在窗格中保持登录状态（Cookie）：Chrome 不允许嵌入在工作台中的网页用脚本读写它自己的大部分 Cookie，因此网站在窗格里可能显示未登录，或者反复出现验证页。对开启了「在窗格中保持登录状态」（默认开启，可按网站关闭）且已授权的网站，Mullion 会在本地通过 chrome.cookies 接口，只在工作台窗格内、只为该网站本身读写它自己的 Cookie，效果与在普通标签页中一致：页面脚本能读到的 Cookie 范围与标签页相同，HttpOnly Cookie 永远不会交给页面；页面写入的 Cookie 按网站自己设定的属性保存。Mullion 不保存 Cookie 副本，不读取其他网站的 Cookie，也不会上传任何 Cookie。普通标签页中的网页不受影响。

命令面板与快捷键：你在命令面板中输入的内容只在本地用于匹配工作台网页、操作和书签，不会保存或上传。选择「用默认搜索引擎搜索」时，Chrome 会用你在浏览器中设置的搜索引擎在新标签页搜索这段文字，搜索由该搜索引擎处理，Mullion 不接收搜索内容。快捷键由 Chrome 管理，Mullion 不记录你的按键。

导入导出：配置码和 JSON 文件都在你的浏览器中生成和读取，内容是你的工作台配置（网址、名称、顺序与各项选项），不含账号信息、站点授权或网页内容。它们只会出现在你自己复制、保存或分享的地方，Mullion 不接收。

价格币种与模板推荐：Mullion 会在本地读取浏览器语言列表和系统时区，用来决定显示人民币价格还是默认币种，以及先推荐「中国」还是「海外」的场景模板。语言列表和时区本身不会上传，结账时只发送所选的币种代码。场景模板打包在扩展中，选择模板不会访问任何网站，保存后工作台才会加载模板中的网页。

2. 登录后：我们收集的数据

登录是可选的。你使用邮箱验证码登录后，我们会收集和保存以下数据：

账号信息邮箱地址、账号 ID、注册和登录时间，以及验证码邮件使用的语言（中文或英文）。
登录凭证一次性邮箱验证码，以及登录后的会话令牌。令牌保存在你的浏览器本地，用于保持登录状态。
同步的配置你的整份工作台配置（内容同第 1 节）、版本号、更新时间，以及一个设备标签（例如“Chrome · macOS”），用于在你的设备之间同步。
会员与付款记录试用期限、会员计划，以及 Stripe 返回的付款记录：付款 ID、结账会话 ID、客户 ID、价格、金额、币种、状态（已支付、已退款、争议中）和付款时间。在推出一次性购买之前订阅的用户，还包括订阅状态和到期时间。
技术日志出于安全和故障排查目的，我们的服务提供商可能会自动记录请求的 IP 地址、时间和浏览器信息。

付款：付款在 Stripe 的结账页面完成。银行卡等支付信息由 Stripe 直接收集和处理，我们无法获取。为了完成付款，我们会把你的邮箱地址和账号 ID 提供给 Stripe。

3. 我们如何使用这些数据

  • 完成登录：向你的邮箱发送验证码，并保持登录状态；
  • 在你登录的设备之间同步工作台配置；
  • 确认你的付费权益（试用、购买、退款或争议状态），处理付款和退款；
  • 回复你的咨询，保障服务安全，防止滥用。

我们不会把你的数据用于广告、用户画像或与上述功能无关的用途，也不会用来判断信用或发放贷款。

4. 数据共享

我们不出售、不出租你的数据。只有在下列情况下，数据才会交给第三方：

  • Supabase：提供账号登录、数据库、验证码邮件发送和服务端函数，存储第 2 节中的数据。参见 Supabase 隐私政策。
  • Stripe：处理付款、退款和争议。参见 Stripe 隐私政策。
  • 法律要求：在法律法规、司法或政府机关依法要求时，我们可能需要提供数据。

这些服务器可能位于你所在的国家或地区之外。

5. 数据安全

扩展与服务端之间的通信全部使用 HTTPS 加密。数据库启用了行级安全策略，你的配置和会员记录只有你本人的账号可以读取。会员资格只能由服务端写入（例如根据 Stripe 的付款结果），扩展无法修改。

6. 保存期限与删除

  • 本地数据会一直保存在你的浏览器中，直到你修改或移除扩展。卸载 Mullion 会清除这些数据。退出登录不会删除本机配置。
  • 云端数据会保存到你的账号被删除为止。你可以发邮件到下方地址申请删除账号：我们会删除你的账号、同步的配置和会员记录。Stripe 会按照其自身政策和法律要求保留交易记录。
  • 你也可以联系我们，查询、更正或导出你的数据。

7. 儿童

Mullion 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策

Mullion 对通过 Chrome API 获得的信息的使用，遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。

本政策更新时，我们会修改本页内容和顶部的生效日期。更新后继续使用 Mullion，即表示你接受更新后的政策。

English

This policy explains how the Chrome extension "Mullion" ("Mullion", "we") collects, uses, stores and discloses information. By using Mullion you acknowledge this policy.

In short: if you don't sign in, everything stays in your browser and we receive nothing. Only if you choose to sign in do we store your email address, your workspace configuration and your membership records, to sync your devices and provide membership. We don't sell data, show ads or use analytics SDKs, and we never collect or upload the content of the pages you view.

1. Without an account: data stays local

The following is kept in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) in your Chrome profile and is never sent to us:

  • Your workspace configuration: the URLs you add, their names and order, and settings such as compatibility mode, external-link handling, zoom and selected regions (an element locator and a short label);
  • Interface state such as draft URLs awaiting site access.

Pages in the workspace are loaded by your browser directly from their websites, whose own privacy policies apply. To fit page width, show a selected region and open external links in new tabs, Mullion runs scripts packaged in the extension inside previews of sites you have granted access to. These scripts only adjust the display locally; they do not read, store or upload page content.

Bookmarks: If you enable the bookmark drawer or bookmark search in the command palette and grant access, Mullion reads your Chrome bookmarks locally to display and search them. It never creates, modifies or uploads bookmarks. Site icons come only from Chrome's local cache; the bookmarked sites are not contacted.

Embedding check: For sites you have granted access to, Mullion reads the response headers of workspace previews (such as X-Frame-Options and Content-Security-Policy) locally, to tell whether a site refuses to be embedded and explain it in the pane. This is used only for display at the time; it is not stored or uploaded. "Report this site" in a pane opens a draft in your own email app containing only the site's domain, and you decide whether to send it.

Scope of site access: Since 1.7, when Mullion asks for access to a website, the request covers the whole site, subdomains and both http and https included (for example "all xueqiu.com sites"). Sites often serve their APIs and sign-in from subdomains, and panes need this to carry your sign-in. You can withdraw access at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

Staying signed in inside panes (cookies): Chrome doesn't let a page embedded in the workspace use script to read or write most of its own cookies, so a site may look signed out in a pane or keep showing a verification page. For granted sites with "Stay signed in inside panes" on (the default; it can be turned off per site), Mullion uses the chrome.cookies API locally, only inside workspace panes and only for that site itself, to read and write the site's own cookies as in a normal tab: the page's scripts see the same cookies they would in a tab, and HttpOnly cookies are never given to the page; cookies the page writes are stored with the attributes the site set. Mullion keeps no copy of cookies, never reads other sites' cookies, and never uploads any cookie. Pages in normal tabs are not affected.

Command palette and shortcuts: What you type in the command palette is used only locally, to match workspace pages, actions and bookmarks; it is not stored or uploaded. If you choose "Search with your default search engine", Chrome searches for that text in a new tab with the search engine set in your browser, which handles the search; Mullion does not receive it. Keyboard shortcuts are managed by Chrome, and Mullion does not record your keystrokes.

Import and export: Codes and JSON files are created and read in your browser. They contain your workspace configuration (URLs, names, order and options), with no account details, site access or page content. They go only where you copy, save or share them; Mullion does not receive them.

Price currency and template suggestions: Mullion reads your browser's language list and time zone locally to decide whether to show prices in CNY or the default currency, and whether to suggest the China or International scene templates first. The language list and time zone are not uploaded; only the chosen currency code is sent at checkout. Scene templates are packaged in the extension: choosing one contacts no website, and the workspace loads its pages only after you save.

2. With an account: what we collect

Signing in is optional. After you sign in with an email code, we collect and store:

AccountEmail address, account ID, sign-up and sign-in times, and the language (Chinese or English) used for sign-in code emails.
CredentialsOne-time email codes, and a session token after sign-in. The token is stored locally in your browser to keep you signed in.
Synced configurationYour whole workspace configuration (as described in section 1), a revision number, the update time, and a device label (for example "Chrome · macOS"), used to sync your devices.
Membership and paymentsTrial period, membership plan, and payment records returned by Stripe: payment ID, checkout session ID, customer ID, price, amount, currency, status (paid, refunded, disputed) and purchase time. For subscribers from before the one-time purchase, also the subscription status and end date.
Technical logsFor security and troubleshooting, our service providers may automatically log the IP address, time and browser information of requests.

Payments: Payments are completed on Stripe's checkout page. Card and other payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe; we never have access to them. To complete a payment, we share your email address and account ID with Stripe.

3. How we use the data

  • Signing you in: sending the email code and keeping you signed in;
  • Syncing your workspace configuration across the devices where you are signed in;
  • Confirming your membership (trial, purchase, refund or dispute status), and handling payments and refunds;
  • Answering your inquiries, keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to these features, nor to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. It is shared with third parties only in these cases:

  • Supabase provides sign-in, the database, sign-in code emails and server functions, and stores the data in section 2. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe processes payments, refunds and disputes. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose data when lawfully required by law, a court or a government authority.

These servers may be located outside your country or region.

5. Security

All traffic between the extension and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. The database uses row-level security, so your configuration and membership records can be read only by your own account. Membership can be written only by the server (for example, from Stripe's payment results); the extension cannot change it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local data stays in your browser until you change it or remove the extension. Uninstalling Mullion clears it. Signing out does not delete the configuration on that device.
  • Cloud data is kept until your account is deleted. To delete your account, email us at the address below: we will delete your account, synced configuration and membership records. Stripe keeps transaction records according to its own policy and legal obligations.
  • You can also contact us to access, correct or export your data.

7. Children

Mullion is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
